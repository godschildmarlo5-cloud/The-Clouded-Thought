How I'm Rockin

Faith · Protection · Identity

You know how he rockin—
he ain't gotta make no threats,
ain't gotta prove he dangerous,
ain't gotta tell the world
who's standing behind him.

God already made it clear.

He walks different
because he knows
he ain't walking alone.

Angels posted around him
where human eyes can't see,
covering every road
his feet decide to travel.

That's why he moves with peace
in places built for panic.

Why he can walk through pressure
without letting pressure
walk through him.

They might see one man,

but Heaven knows
there's an army around him.

Not an army carrying weapons—
an army carrying purpose.

An army sent to guard
what God hasn't finished building.

Every door that closed
wasn't always rejection.

Sometimes it was protection.

Every person who walked away
wasn't always a loss.

Sometimes God was removing
what he couldn't see clearly.

Every time danger came close
and somehow missed him,
he calls that grace.

Every time the road looked finished
and another path appeared,
he calls that God.

He doesn't move like
nothing can happen to him.

He moves knowing
that whatever happens,
he doesn't have to face it alone.

That's different.

That's faith with a backbone.

He knows people can switch.

Friends can fade.

Promises can break.

But God's protection
doesn't depend on somebody
keeping their word.

So he keeps his head high
and his heart grounded.

No need to chase respect.

No need to force loyalty.

No need to convince anybody
that he's protected.

The evidence is in his survival.

Look at everything
that should've made him quit.

Look at everything
that tried to bury his purpose.

He's still here.

Still thinking.

Still creating.

Still dreaming.

Still becoming.

That's why he smiles sometimes
when life gets loud.

Because somewhere beyond
what his eyes can see,
he believes Heaven is moving.

Angels don't need applause.

God doesn't need witnesses
to prove what He's doing.

And he doesn't need
to explain every blessing.

He just keeps walking.

Covered.

Guided.

Protected.

Not because he's untouchable,

but because his life
is bigger than the moments
that tried to break him.

So when he steps outside,
he isn't stepping out alone.

When he enters a room,
he carries something with him
that money can't buy
and fear can't take.

Peace.

Purpose.

Faith.

And a protection
he can't always see
but refuses to doubt.

You know how he rockin.

He ain't gotta tell everybody
who's with him.

He already knows.

God in front.

Angels around him.

Faith inside him.

And an entire army of Heaven
moving where his eyes
can't follow.
